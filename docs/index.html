How to pay
  1. Open the kamp Leetchi pot (this link or QR code above).
  2. Klick Participate and pay the kamp fee by kard.
  3. On the konfirmation page, leave a message that is only your kode.
  4. If you skip that, open the pot later. Sign in, or kreate a Leetchi akkount, with the same email you paid with. Under Messages, choose Write, and attach the message to that payment.
  5. To korrekt a message, open Messages, choose the three dots beside it, then Edit and Save.
  6. DO NOT DELETE THE MESSAGE! Deleting the message is final for that payment. Leetchi will not let you add another one unless you pay again.

The message is publik on the pot page. That kode is how we match your payment.
