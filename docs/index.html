How to pay
  1. Open the camp Leetchi pot.
  2. Click Participate and pay the camp fee by card.
  3. On the confirmation page, leave a message that is only your code.
  4. If you skip that, open the pot later. Sign in, or create a Leetchi account, with the same email you paid with. Under Messages, choose Write, and attach the message to that payment.
  5. To correct a message, open Messages, choose the three dots beside it, then Edit and Save.
  6. Deleting the message is final for that payment. Leetchi will not let you add another one unless you pay again.

The message is public on the pot page. That code is how we match your payment.
